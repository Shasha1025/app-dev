# My Favorite Series: 18 Again

> "No matter how hard life gets, never regret anything that made you smile." — Hong Dae-young

## Overview
**18 Again** is a 2020 South Korean fantasy coming-of-age drama series based on the 2009 American film *17 Again*. Starring **Lee Do-hyun**, the drama tells the story of a 37-year-old man on the verge of divorce who suddenly finds himself transformed back into his 18-year-old body while retaining his adult mindset.

---

## Key Details
* **Genre:** Fantasy / Rom-Com / Family / Coming-of-Age
* **Original Release:** September 21, 2020 – November 10, 2020
* **Director:** Ha Byung-hoon
* **Network:** JTBC

---

## Main Characters
1. **Go Woo-young / Young Hong Dae-young** (played by *Lee Do-hyun*) - The 18-year-old version of Dae-young who gets a second chance at youth and reconnects with his children as a classmate.
2. **Jung Da-jung** (played by *Kim Ha-neul*) - Dae-young's wife, an aspiring anchorwoman pursuing her dreams.
3. **Hong Dae-young** (played by *Yoon Sang-hyun*) - The 37-year-old version of Dae-young facing career and marriage struggles.

---

## Why It's Great
* **Lee Do-hyun's Performance:** Masterfully portrays a 37-year-old father trapped inside an 18-year-old high schooler's body.
* **Heartwarming Themes:** Deeply explores family reconciliation, second chances, and young love.
* **Emotional Soundtrack:** Features touching OST tracks that elevate key dramatic moments.
